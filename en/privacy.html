Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 7, 2026

This is an English translation provided for reference. If there is any difference between this translation and the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

BMTX (“we,” “us,” or “our”) sets out this Privacy Policy (this “Policy”) as follows regarding how we handle your information in the journal app “hibi” (the “App”).

1.Introduction

What hibi values most is “being able to keep records for yourself, with peace of mind.”

The contents of your journal, such as the text, photos, videos, location information, tags, and moods that you record in the App, are stored only on your device and in your own Google Drive that you have connected yourself.

We have no means of accessing the text, photos, or videos of your journal. This is because we have no server that stores your records in the first place.

What we can know is limited to anonymous usage statistics (metadata such as which screens were displayed and which buttons were tapped) that we collect to improve the App. See below for details.

2.Information Stored on Your Device (We Do Not View or Collect It)

The following information is stored on your device and is never sent to our servers. We cannot view or collect its contents.

  • The text, tags, moods, and recorded date and time of your entries
  • Photos and videos (those you select or take and attach)
  • Location information (obtained and stored only when you use “Place” to add a location while writing an entry. We do not obtain your location continuously or in the background.)
  • If you sign in with Google, the name, email address, and profile picture of your Google account, and the credentials for accessing Google Drive
  • Notification settings (whether “On This Day” and “Daily Reminder” are on or off, and their times)
  • Some records of how you use the App (records of actions such as tapping notifications and writing entries; some of these are sent anonymously as the usage statistics described in Section 3)

This information is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App. If you turn on backup, it is also stored in your own Google Drive (see Section 4).

3.Information We Collect to Improve the App (Anonymous Usage Statistics Only)

To improve the App, we send the following anonymous usage statistics to a third-party product analytics service and analyze them.

  • App launches and screen views
  • The number of times each feature is used (creating and editing entries, running backups, tapping notifications, etc.)
  • Snapshots of your settings (notifications on/off, whether OS notification permission is granted, etc.)

These usage statistics do not include any information that identifies you, such as the text, photos, or videos of your journal, the place names or coordinates of location information, or your name or email address. What we know is limited to metadata such as “which screen was opened” and “which button was tapped,” and we cannot know what you have written.

We do not use this information for advertising purposes, and there is no tracking by third-party advertising companies.

Details of the information sent to the analytics service

For transparency, we disclose below the information we send for analyzing the usage statistics described above.

If the recipient service changes, we will revise this Policy and make it public.

4.Google Sign-In and Backups Using Google Drive

If you use Google sign-in in the App, we store the name, email address, and profile picture of your Google account and the credentials for accessing Google Drive on your device. These are used to save your journal to, and restore it from, your own Google Drive.

If you turn on backup, your journal data (a set of the text, tags, moods, location, date and time, and so on) and the attached photos and videos are saved in the hibi-backup folder in your own Google Drive. This is a storage location that you manage, and you can view or delete it yourself at any time. This does not constitute provision to any third party, including us.

The App’s retrieval, storage, and use of information received from Google APIs (including Google Drive) adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, information received from the hibi-backup folder in your Google Drive is used only to provide the features you have explicitly allowed (backup and restore), and is not used for advertising, read by humans (except in very limited cases such as security investigations or legal compliance, or with your explicit consent), or transferred to third parties.

When you use the authentication and storage features provided by Google, Google’s privacy policy applies.

5.Permissions Used by the App

To provide its features, the App may ask you for the following OS permissions. Each is requested through an OS dialog when you use the relevant feature, and you can continue to use the App even if you do not allow it (some features will not be available).

  • Camera: Used when you take photos or videos and attach them to an entry while writing it.
  • Photo library: Used when you select existing photos or videos and attach them to an entry while writing it, or when you set a profile picture.
  • Location: Your device’s location is obtained only when you use “Place” to add a location while writing an entry. We do not obtain your location continuously or in the background. The obtained location is converted through reverse geocoding (converting coordinates into a place name) and stored on your device, linked to that entry. If you turn on backup, it is also stored in your own Google Drive (see Section 4).
  • Notifications: Used to deliver “On This Day” and “Daily Reminder.” All notifications from the App are local notifications generated on your device, and they are not delivered from our servers. You can change whether notifications are on or off, and their times, at any time in the App’s settings.

6.Link Previews

If a URL is written in the text of an entry, the App may connect to that site when displaying the entry in order to fetch a preview (title and image). Only the URL and general connection information are sent through this connection; the text, photos, and videos of your journal are never sent.

7.Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide or sell the contents of your journal (text, photos, videos, location information, tags, and moods) to any third party.

As described in Section 4, backups to Google Drive are saved within your own Google account and do not constitute provision to a third party.

We send the anonymous usage statistics described in Section 3 to a third-party product analytics service, but they do not include the contents of your journal or any information that identifies you.

8.Deleting Data and Your Rights

You can do the following with your data.

  • Deleting individual entries: You can delete them in the App at any time.
  • Deleting all data: In the App, go to Settings → “Delete All Data” to delete all entries, photos, videos, settings, and credentials on your device at once. The App’s authorization (access rights) to the connected Google account is also revoked.
  • Disconnecting Google: In the App, go to Settings → “Backup” → “Sign Out” to delete the credentials on your device and stop access to Google Drive.
  • Deleting backups on Google Drive: You can erase your backup data by deleting the hibi-backup folder in your Google Drive (you do this yourself, outside the App).
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the App deletes all data on your device (please delete backups on Google Drive separately using the steps above).

For inquiries about your data or requests for deletion, please contact us using the contact details below.

9.Security

To prevent unauthorized access by third parties, the App provides an App Lock feature (passcode and biometrics).

10.Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for use by people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please use the App with the consent of your parent or guardian.

11.Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as necessary. When we make an important change, we will notify you in the App or on this page. The revised Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
